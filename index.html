    What this can and cannot do

    Everything here is measured from a flat picture on one camera. Ratios, angles within the frame, and how a swing changes shot to shot are all solid. Anything converted into real-world units — club speed, ball speed, distances in centimetres — is an estimate scaled from your height, and reads low when movement is towards or away from the camera. It is a mirror with a memory, not a launch monitor.
